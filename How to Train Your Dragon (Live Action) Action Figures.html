The basic dragons for the live-action are a bit random, since there is no Stormfly, Hookfang, etc. Instead, they included background dragons like the Rumblehorn. I really like that they’ve included the Skrill, Rumblehorn, and Terrible Terrors. The figures have nice details, and I like all of them, but Toothless looks a bit stupid and comes across as rather cheap compared to the others. The Terrible Terrors and the green Gronckle haven’t arrived in my country yet, so they’re difficult to get for now. But I’m really excited to get them!
